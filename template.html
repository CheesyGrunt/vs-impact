How these numbers are built

Figures come directly from the Virtual Services engagement log. Volunteer time value uses the Independent Sector value of volunteer time for the year in which the hours were served. In-kind value is self-reported by volunteers at the point of logging.

Recipient names, volunteer names, and any individual record detail are deliberately excluded. Everything shown here is aggregated.

Read the current year carefully

2026 is a partial year and will keep moving. Use the January through May comparison for any year-over-year claim.

A small number of logged engagements are flagged as potential duplicates or entry errors during review and are held out of supported counts.
